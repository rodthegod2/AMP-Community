# AMP Community

A community fork of [AMP (Adammantium Multiplayer Mod)](https://github.com/AdammantiumMultiplayer/AMP) for Blade & Sorcery, with stability fixes, hosting from the game on PC (the router port opens by itself, friends join with a short code), mod sync, settings sync and an in-game updater.

- Downloads: [GitHub releases](https://github.com/rodthegod2/AMP-Community/releases)
- How to install, host and join: [docs/HOW-TO-PLAY.txt](docs/HOW-TO-PLAY.txt)
- PC (Steam). Quest (Nomad) support is experimental and not yet tested on a headset: Quest players get the host's mods from mod.io (the host's PC finds Quest versions of its mods), see section 8 of the how-to.
